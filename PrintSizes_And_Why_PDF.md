## 🖨️ Why Sellers Deliver as PDFs

Etsy sellers usually sell **digital files in PDF format** for these reasons:

1. **Preserves Quality**

   * PDFs can store **vector-based or high-res raster images** without compression issues like JPG.
   * Keeps artwork crisp for printing at *any* size offered.

2. **Print-Shop Friendly**

   * Print shops often prefer PDFs — easier to scale, ensures correct dimensions, no color profile mess-ups.

3. **Multi-Page Convenience**

   * One PDF can contain multiple sizes (page 1 = 8x10, page 2 = 11x14, etc.).
   * Buyers don’t have to download 10 separate files.

4. **Cross-Platform Compatibility**

   * Works on Windows, Mac, mobile, and with any standard printer software.

5. **Professional Perception**

   * Feels more “polished” and “print-ready” compared to just handing over JPGs or PNGs.
